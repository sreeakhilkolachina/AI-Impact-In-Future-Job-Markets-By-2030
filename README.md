# AI Impact In Future Job Markets From 2026 - 2030

Overview of the Project:

• Created an interactive dashboard using Microsoft Power BI platform / tool. The dashboard is prepared for the analysis, comparisons and identifying the trends of AI Impact on Job Market from 2026 – 2030. Which is titled as AI Impact in Future Job Markets By 2030. The DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) are used in this project to create calculated measures and calculated column. The dataset used here is in CSV (Comma - Separated Values) format. Which is sourced from Kaggle (Public Dataset). 

The project consist of three interactive dashboards / sheets.

Dashboard / Sheet – 1: Title Page. 

Dashboard / Sheet – 2: Overall Overview. 

Dashboard / Sheet – 3: AI Impact Analysis.


Introduction:

• Artificial Intelligence has become one of the most transformative technologies in today's world. Organizations and Industries are adopting to AI - powered tools and automations to improve efficiency, enhance productivity, and support data – driven and decision – making. While these advancements create new opportunities, but it also introduce challenges such as workforce transformation, changing job requirements, skill gaps, and concerns regarding job displacement.

• So to understand these technologies, advancements, opportunities and challenges, this project AI Impact In Future Job Market By 2030 consisting of interactive dashboards which
provides trends from 2026 – 2030. Where user can get meaningful insights, trends and comparisons across various sectors and fields. The dashboard consist of visualizations which
are easy understand.

• The main aim of this project lies on the relationship between AI and employment, by comparing AI Replacement Risk, Future Demand Scores, Automation Trends, AI Tools
Usage, Remote Work Adoption, and Workforce Readiness. This project supports for Business Analytics and much better understanding of AI and its impact.
